We’re building the infrastructure for the world’s first global financial computer. Whether you’re running a prop trading firm, building the next DeFi protocol, or pioneering entirely new financial primitives, Helius gives you the tools to turn ambitious ideas into reality. We don’t settle for “good enough.” We build infrastructure that entire markets depend on. Helius is Solana’s leading infrastructure provider, purpose-built by a Solana-native team with deep protocol expertise. From high-performance RPC nodes and transaction sending (Sender) to gRPC streaming (LaserStream), webhooks, and digital asset APIs — Helius powers teams like Phantom, Jupiter, DFlow, Coinbase, and Bitwise. Founded in 2022 and backed by Founders Fund, Haun Ventures, Foundation Capital, and angel investors including Anatoly Yakovenko, Helius processes billions of daily requests with 99.99% uptime. Build the future of Solana with Helius.
