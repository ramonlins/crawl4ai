##  Getting Started with Crypto Payments
Helius allows you to pay for plans using USDC on Solana. When a payment is due, you’ll receive a payment link and can complete the transaction directly from your wallet. Below, you’ll find an easy-to-follow guide on how to get started.
###  Purchasing a Plan with Crypto
Navigate to the Billing Page
Go to <https://dashboard.helius.dev/billing> and select the subscription plan that fits your needs (e.g., Business). Choose either **monthly** or **annual** billing.
Initiate Crypto Payment
Click **Pay with Crypto** to start the checkout process.
Enter Billing Information
Provide your **name** and **billing address**.
Complete Payment
Connect your wallet or scan the QR code and complete the payment in USDC. Once your transaction is confirmed on-chain, your new plan will be active.
**Important Notes** :
  * **Payment Links** : When a payment is due, you’ll receive a payment link by email and in your dashboard.
  * **USDC on Solana** : All crypto payments are made in USDC on Solana.
  * **Cancel Anytime** : You can cancel your subscription if needed.


##  Frequently Asked Questions
How do crypto payments work?
When a payment is due, you’ll receive a payment link by email and in your [dashboard](https://dashboard.helius.dev/billing). Open the link, connect your wallet or scan the QR code, and complete the payment in USDC. Once the transaction is confirmed on-chain, your payment is applied and your subscription continues.
I want to switch between fiat and crypto payments.
Please contact our [support](https://dashboard.helius.dev/support) team, and they will assist you with changing your payment method.
I paid for a plan, but it hasn't been updated yet in my dashboard.
It can take up to **1 hour** for the system to update your plan. If it still doesn’t update after that, reach out to our [support](https://dashboard.helius.dev/support) team.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel by going to the [**Billing**](https://dashboard.helius.dev/billing) page and clicking the **Cancel Subscription** button.
