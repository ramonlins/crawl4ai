# Dota 2 Patch 7.19b
# Source: https://www.dota2.com/patches/7.19b

Gameplay Update
7.19b
General Updates
Buyback respawn time penalty changed from +25% of remaining time to +25s always
  

General:Default:Level 2 XP requirement changed from 200 to 230 (in a scenario where Team A gets 2 melee denies per wave, and team B gets 1 melee deny per wave: Team A wave requirement for level 2 increases from 2 waves to 3 waves. Team B wave requirement remains at 3 waves. Total XP needed for other levels unchanged.)
  

Glyph is now on cooldown until the 3 minute timer in-game
