# Dota 2 Patch 7.39b
# Source: https://www.dota2.com/patches/7.39b

Gameplay Update
7.39b
General Updates
Terrain Changes
The Bottom Radiant Tier 1 tower has been moved slightly to the south
Several trees have been added and adjusted above the Bottom Radiant Tier 1 tower and on the west side of the lane
Adjusted a juke path to the east of the Mid Radiant Tier 2 tower
A tree has been removed to the south of the Top Dire Tier 1 tower
Fixed some locations that were incorrectly blocked for warding
Neutral Creep Updates
Item Updates
Neutral Item Updates
Artifact changes
Jidi Pollen Bag
Pollinate health restoration loss increased from 20% to 30%
  

Dezun Bloodrite
Abilities that are considered attacks are no longer impacted by Dezun Bloodrite (Including Outworld Destroyer's Arcane Orb, Viper's Poison Attack, etc.)
Blood Invocation AoE bonus decreased from 15% to 12% 
Dormant Curio AoE bonus decreased from 19.5% to 15.6%
Giant's Maul
Crushing Blow debuff duration decreased from 4s to 3s
Magnifying Monocle
Keen Eye bonus cast range decreased from 125 to 100 
Dormant Curio cast range bonus decreased from 162.5 to 130
  

Helm of the Undying
Death Delay cooldown increased from 50s to 100s
Enchantment changes
Crude
Health Restoration bonus increased from +20/30% to +30/40%
Intelligence reduction decreased from 8% to 5%
