# Dota 2 Patch 7.39c
# Source: https://www.dota2.com/patches/7.39c

Gameplay Update
7.39c
General Updates
Map Objectives
Roshan: Melee attacks against couriers are now treated as melee hero attacks
Tormentor: Reflect no longer considers creep-heroes for the damage reflected 
Lone Druid's Spirit Bear is an exception for this
  

Terrain Changes
Watchers by the respective Wisdom shrines have been slightly moved away from the nearby ramps
Watcher activation range decreased from 300 to 200
Neutral Creep Updates
Boglet
Arm of the Deep: Cast point increased from 0.2s to 0.3s
Croaker
Tendrils of the Deep: Cast point increased from 0.2s to 0.3s
Ancient Croaker
Congregation of the Deep: Cast point increased from 0.2s to 0.3s
Ancient Prowler Shaman
Petrify: No longer has a health threshold restriction when the Shaman is controlled by a player
Item Updates
Orb of Venom
Poison Attack no longer has True Strike on the attack
Blade Mail
Armor bonus decreased from +7 to +6
Hurricane Pike
Hurricane Thrust buff provided to the wearer when targeting an enemy is now dispellable
Harpoon
Draw Forth initial projectile is now disjointable
Phylactery
Recipe cost decreased from 400 to 300. Total cost decreased from 2600 to 2500
Khanda
Total cost decreased from 5700 to 5600 (due to Phylactery Recipe cost reduction)
Empower Spell Break debuff no longer dispellable 
Slow debuff applied by Empower Spell is still dispellable
Neutral Item Updates
Artifact changes
Ripper's Lash
Flay cast range increased from 700 to 850
Mana Draught
Bottoms Up mana restore rescaled from 60 + 4% to 70 + 3% 
Dormant Curio Max Mana Restoration decreased from 5.2% to 3.9%
Searing Signet
Burn Through no longer applies if the wearer is more than 2000 range away from the target
Serrated Shiv
Gut 'Em no longer grants Illusions the chance to True Strike
Giant's Maul
Crushing Blow can no longer be applied by illusions
Enchantment changes
Vampiric
Spell Lifesteal bonus decreased from 8/10/12% to 6/8/10%
