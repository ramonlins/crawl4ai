Q2: Beware of fake adminis private messages. GMGN group admins will never DM you private messages first.
1) Recently, many users have been sent private messages by scammers pretending to be administrators, claiming to solve problems. GMGN official administrators will never take the initiative to send private messages to anyone, nor will they ask you for private keys. 2) Case of fraudulent account disguised management: hide the user name (user name area), write the real administrator's username in the introduction to confuse users, and commit fraud. Q3: How to Open the Browser Console to Assist GMGN in Resolving Your Issue
Step 1: Record the Issue
  * Take a full-screen screenshot of the problem, preferably record a video.


Step 2: Open Console and Take Screenshots
  * Right-click a blank area of the webpage in the browser, select “Inspect.” It will automatically open Developer Tools; click “Console” 
  * Drag the Console area larger, and look for red error messages (marked red or labeled “Error”)
  * Take screenshots including all red errors, yellow warnings, prioritizing the earliest error, and capture as much content as possible


Step 3: Contact Support
  * Send the screenshots to customer support, or DM the official Twitter account.

Q4: Webpage No Sound Issue
If you've already checked the Chrome webpage sound toggle, GMGN notification settings, and the notification sound switches for various functions in the bottom left corner of the GMGN webpage, but there’s still no sound, you can follow these steps to resolve the issue:
  * Go to Browser Settings: Settings / Privacy and Security / Site Settings / Additional Content Settings / Sound
  * Click the "Add" button next to "Allow to play sound," enter gmgn.ai and confirm.

Q5: The wallet was stolen or the balance of the wallet was emptied
Common situation: 1) Whether Android phone, it has clicked an unknown link before, resulting in a Trojan horse or malicious program in the mobile phone/computer. 2) Whether Telegram app is downloaded through official sources; whether third-party Telegram Translator plugin from unknown sources is downloaded. 3) Whether Telegram has authorized an unfamiliar fake bot and the balance in the wallet has been transferred by the scam bot by entering a cell phone number or clicking on a scam link, and it is recommended to identify the same through multiple official GMGN channels before making transactions. 4) The wallet has authorized the scam app, you can enter the wallet to query the authorization list and cancel the unknown authorization. _e.g: some scammers use NFT mint places to win prizes and attract you to sign authorization on the website._ 5) If you need to import or export the private key, it is recommended to partially copy and partially manually enter it to reduce the risk of theft due to Trojan reading the clipboard. ⚠️GMGN's first purpose is security, there is no loophole that leads to the transfer of wallet balances, and in case of theft, please check yourself according to the above-listed situations. GMGN does not perform any operations on the user's wallet, and we do not have the ability to transfer any user's wallet balance. We fully understand the feelings of being stolen, the reason for theft is basically the above mentioned: equipment security problems, the use of fake fraud bot, tg abnormal authorization, etc. These are GMGN official can not control, at present we can only give you some self-checking advice, and tell how to reduce the subsequent risk.
